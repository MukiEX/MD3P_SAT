2Options

Mouse speed

Shuttle Mouse travel per column. ×1 is 32 counts per column.

Glide

Draw the clown and aim line at the paddle position, up to one column either side.

Pulse target column

The balloons in the column under the paddle brighten and dim on a 48-frame cycle.

X pulls down

In play, X pulls the field down instead of catching, like C on the Neo Geo. A and C still catch; menus are unchanged.

Pure paddle

Menus never use mouse Y. Off: a mouse counts as a spinner (X only) until it moves up or down a fair bit, then menus use both axes.

Spinner on a pad's d-pad: latch on first diagonal

An encoder inside a real pad goes across the d-pad pairs (phase A on LEFT or RIGHT, phase B on UP or DOWN; a real pad drops off the bus while LEFT+RIGHT or UP+DOWN are held). It is found automatically after a quick spin. This switch latches it on the first diagonal instead; leave off for normal pads.

Magnet

Half mouse speed over a column you can catch from; wheels and sticks get 4× hysteresis there.

What gets written

Only 0.BIN changes: 13 sectors of Track 01, each with fresh Mode 1 EDC/ECC. The patched Track 01 has CRC32 9800971B with default options.

CHD files are decoded in full to verify Track 01 and to recompute the raw and overall SHA-1. Unchanged hunks are copied byte for byte; the changed ones are stored uncompressed.

Need BIN/CUE from a CHD? Convert with chdman extractcd first, then patch Track 01.
